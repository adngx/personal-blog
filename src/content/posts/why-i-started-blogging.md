Everyone says "build in public." I thought that meant posting side projects on Twitter. It turns out writing is the real accelerator.

## The Feynman technique, but online

Richard Feynman said if you cannot explain something simply, you do not understand it well enough. A blog post is that test, published. When I write about a concept, I find the gaps in my understanding fast.

## Who is this for?

Two audiences, and that is intentional:

1. **Students like me** who are just starting out and want to see how someone at the same level thinks through problems.
2. **Senior engineers** who can spot where my reasoning is wrong and (hopefully) drop a correction.

Both groups make me better. The students keep me honest about clarity. The seniors keep me honest about correctness.

## What I plan to cover

- Things I learn in my CS courses
- Projects I build and what goes wrong
- Concepts that took me a while to grasp

No hot takes. No engagement bait. Just a high school student learning computer science, writing it down as I go.
